## 1. DETAILED CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE

### June 5, 2024 — The Spark `[VERIFIED]` (L1-01)
- **Event:** The High Court Division reinstated a quota reserving 30% of public-sector jobs for descendants of independence fighters. It ruled the 2018 abolition of the quota system unconstitutional (SRC-OHCHR para. 19; SRC-JA).
- **Not established here:** the "56% total quota" figure in the earlier draft. No source read for this pass states it.

### July 1 – 7, 2024 — Protests resume; the "Bangla Blockade" `[CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE]` (L1-02, L1-03)
- **Event:**
  - Students resumed protests on 1 July, with blockades at Shahbagh on 2–3 July (SRC-JA).
  - The "Bangla Blockade" began on 6 July, and Dhaka was largely at a standstill on 7 July (SRC-JA; SRC-EUAA heading "7–10 July").
  - The platform was **Students Against Discrimination** (named in SRC-OHCHR para. 41 and SRC-PA-1POINT).
- **Locations:**
  - Shahbagh and other Dhaka junctions are sourced.
  - Specific campus spots (Curzon Hall, Central Library) are **RECONSTRUCTION** in the game.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 1 — Where It All Began**.

### July 8 – 13, 2024 — Blockades continue; first attacks `[CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE]`
- **Events:**
  - 8 July: blockades at eleven Dhaka points.
  - 10 July: the Appellate Division ordered a four-week status quo.
  - 11 July: police used batons and tear gas near Cumilla University (SRC-JA; SRC-OHCHR para. 32).
- **Removed:** "zero property damage reported", which no source read here supports.

### July 14, 2024 — The "razakar" remark `[VERIFIED]` (L1-07)
- **Event:** At a press conference the Prime Minister said: "If the grandchildren of the freedom fighters don't get quota benefits, should the grandchildren of razakars get the benefit?" (OHCHR translation, SRC-OHCHR para. 33).
- **Documented chant (English, per SRC-OHCHR para. 34):**
  - "Who are you? Who am I? Razakar, razakar."
  - The chant was *later* expanded with "Says who? Says who? The dictator, the dictator!"
  - At Dhaka University, hundreds of women broke the night locks of their halls to join.
- **Bangla/romanised spelling** (তুমি কে? আমি কে? রাজাকার, রাজাকার … কে বলেছে? কে বলেছে? স্বৈরাচার, স্বৈরাচার): `[CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE]`. This form was seen only on a tertiary page and is not yet checked against a primary Bangla source.

### July 15, 2024 — Attacks at Dhaka University and DMCH `[VERIFIED]` (L1-08)
- **Event:**
  - Chhatra League supporters, "armed with machetes, axes, sticks, motorbike helmets for protection and some pistols", attacked student protesters at Dhaka University after 15:00. Women were struck and threatened, and police made "no meaningful effort" to intervene (SRC-OHCHR para. 85).
  - In the early evening, Chhatra League supporters stormed emergency units at Dhaka Medical College Hospital (para. 86).
- **Injuries:** "more than 300 injured on both sides" (SRC-DS-TL). The earlier "over 300 students hospitalized" is not supported and has been corrected.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 2 — The Day the Streets Changed**.

### July 16, 2024 — Abu Sayed killed in Rangpur `[VERIFIED]` (L3-01, L3-03)
- **Event:** At Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur, Abu Sayed raised his arms as police began shooting. Two officers about 14–15 m away fired shotguns loaded with metal pellets, at least twice. OHCHR found "a deliberate extrajudicial killing by the police" (SRC-OHCHR Case 1; SRC-UNBD).
- **Impact:** footage of his killing "mobilized more protesters" (SRC-OHCHR para. 37). At least six people were killed that day (para. 37; SRC-DS-TL).
- **Corrections:**
  - The earlier "point-blank range" is contradicted by OHCHR (14–15 m) and has been removed.
  - His age is `[DISPUTED / UNCONFIRMED]`: 23 per OHCHR, 25 per July Archive (L3-02).

### July 17 – 18, 2024 — "Complete Shutdown" and lethal force `[VERIFIED]`
- **Events:**
  - 17 July: police, RAB and BGB suppressed a protest at Dhaka University, and students called a "complete shutdown" (SRC-OHCHR paras. 39 and 41; SRC-DS-TL).
  - From 18 July the general population joined, and security forces "switched to a lethal force paradigm" (SRC-OHCHR para. 43).
- **Mir Mahfuzur Rahman Mugdho `[VERIFIED]` (L3-04, L3-05):**
  - In Uttara on 18 July, Mugdho, a 25-year-old student, was handing out water to protesters when he was killed by gunfire from security forces, at around 17:50 on Rabindra Sarani (SRC-OHCHR Case 3; SRC-DS-MUGDHO; SRC-BSS-MUGDHO).
  - His call is recorded as "পানি লাগবে কারো, পানি, পানি?" (SRC-DSB-MUGDHO).
  - This file deliberately does not describe his wound.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 3 — A City on Edge**. Level 3 is set at Dhaka University / Shahbagh, **not** Uttara, and is not a Mugdho scene.

### July 18 (evening) – July 23, 2024 — Internet shutdown and curfew `[VERIFIED]` (L1-09, L4-01, L4-02)
- **Internet:** a complete internet shutdown was imposed on the evening of 18 July and lasted until 23 July (SRC-OHCHR para. 44). Mobile data had already been cut around campuses from 14–15 July (para. 193).
- **Curfew:**
  - A national curfew took effect from midnight on 19 July, and 27,000 Army soldiers were deployed (SRC-OHCHR para. 44).
  - On 19 July, minister Obaidul Quader told reporters that forces had orders to "shoot on sight" (SRC-OHCHR para. 117).
- **Numbers `[CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE]` (L4-05):**
  - OHCHR estimates about 100 deaths on 18 July and close to 300 on 19 July (para. 118).
  - Police reported arresting 10,525 men and 25 women from 1 July to 4 August, and RAB a further 1,152 (para. 160).
  - These replace the earlier unsourced "over 200 killed during the blackout" and "over 10,000 arrested in midnight sweeps".
- **Helicopters (L4-06):**
  - Tear gas and sound grenades dropped from RAB and police helicopters: `[VERIFIED]`.
  - Gunfire from helicopters: `[DISPUTED / UNCONFIRMED]`. OHCHR "cannot confirm or exclude" it (para. 144).
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 4 — Curfew & Covert Routes**.

### July 21 – August 2, 2024 — Court ruling, arrests, renewed protest `[VERIFIED]`
- **Events:**
  - 21 July: the Supreme Court capped the freedom-fighter quota at 5%, and quotas overall at 7% (SRC-OHCHR para. 45; SRC-DS-TL).
  - 26–28 July: coordinators were taken into Detective Branch custody, and a coerced video statement was published on 28 July (SRC-OHCHR para. 46; SRC-DS-TL).
- **Removed:** "red profile photos", "Swadhinotar Lal" graffiti. No source was read for these in this pass, so they are not used in game text.

### August 3, 2024 — The One-Point Demand `[VERIFIED]` (L5-01)
- **Event:** At around 5:30 pm at the Central Shaheed Minar, coordinator Nahid Islam announced the one-point demand: "the downfall of Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina and the incumbent government" (SRC-PA-1POINT).
- **Crowd size `[CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE]` (L5-02):** Prothom Alo reports "thousands", with the crowd spreading into adjacent areas. The earlier "hundreds of thousands … largest peaceful rally in modern Bangladesh history" is not supported and has been removed.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 5 — Mass Mobilization & The Wall of People**.

### August 4, 2024 — Non-cooperation `[VERIFIED]` (L5-03)
- **Event:** The non-cooperation programme began. About 93–97 people were killed that day per The Daily Star and Prothom Alo, an indefinite curfew was declared, and a "March to Dhaka" was called (SRC-EUAA 4–5 Aug; SRC-JA).
- **Removed:** "citizens withhold taxes and utility payments". No source was read for this in this pass.

### August 5, 2024 ("36 July") — Resignation and departure `[VERIFIED]` (L6-01, L6-03, L6-05)
- **Event:**
  - Hundreds of thousands marched towards central Dhaka, and police and armed Awami League supporters fired on protesters in many places (SRC-OHCHR para. 52).
  - At around 2 pm, Sheikh Hasina "was flown out of Dhaka by Armed Forces helicopter and then out of the country". Protesters stormed her official residence shortly after (para. 53).
  - Reuters reported that she landed at Hindon near Delhi (SRC-KT-REUTERS).
- **Army `[CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE]` (L6-02):** the Army and BGB "largely stood by", though soldiers did shoot at Jamuna Future Park (para. 52). The earlier "Armed forces announce they will not fire on the public" is not a direct quote of any source read here.
- **Departure point and route `[DISPUTED / UNCONFIRMED]` (L6-04):** no source read here names the take-off point. The game must not imply that the departure happened at any in-game location.
- **Cultural name `[VERIFIED]`:** protesters kept counting July until their demand was met, so 5 August became **36 July** (SRC-FE-36JULY).
- **Death toll `[VERIFIED as an estimate]` (L6-06):** OHCHR estimates "as many as 1,400" protest-related deaths from 15 July to 5 August (SRC-OHCHR para. 58).
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 6 — 36 July: Historical Turning Point & Epilogue**.
