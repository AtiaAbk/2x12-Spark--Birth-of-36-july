## 1. DETAILED CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE

### June 5, 2024 — The Spark
- **Event:** The High Court Division of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh invalidates a 2018 government circular that abolished quotas in civil service recruitment, effectively reinstating a 30% quota for descendants of freedom fighters.
- **Significance:** Widespread student concern erupts over meritocracy in public job recruitment, where over 56% of civil service positions were reserved under various quotas.

### July 1 – 7, 2024 — Genesis of "Students Against Discrimination"
- **Event:** Public university students across Dhaka University, Jahangirnagar University, and Chittagong University initiate peaceful protest rallies. Formation of *Boishammo Birodhi Chhatra Andolon* (Students Against Discrimination).
- **Locations:** Curzon Hall, Dhaka University Central Library, Shahbagh intersection.
- **Methodology:** Strictly non-violent road blockades termed the "Bangla Blockade", demanding executive reform rather than judicial delays.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 1 — Where It All Began (Peaceful Campus)**.

### July 8 – 13, 2024 — Expansion of the Bangla Blockade
- **Event:** Nationwide road and railway blockades expand to include private university and college students. Demonstrators organize cultural performances, poetry readings, and human chains.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep solidarity across diverse student bodies; zero property damage reported.

### July 14, 2024 — The Turning Point of Insult
- **Event:** A press conference remarks comparing protesters to historical collaborators (*Razakars*) triggers immediate spontaneous protests at midnight across all Dhaka University halls.
- **Documented Chant:** *"Tumi ke? Ami ke? Razakar, Razakar! Ke boleche? Ke boleche? Shwairachar, Shwairachar!"* (Who are you? Who am I? Razakar, Razakar! Who said it? Who said it? The Dictator, the Dictator!)
- **Significance:** The derogatory labeling transforms a socio-economic policy demand into a moral struggle for human dignity and democratic rights.

### July 15, 2024 — The Campus Assault
- **Event:** Ruling party student wing cadres (Bangladesh Chhatra League), reinforced by outside muscle armed with sticks, rods, and helmets, launch violent attacks on peaceful demonstrators at DU (Raju Memorial Sculpture, Mall Chottor, Shahidullah Hall). Female students and medical triage units at Dhaka Medical College Hospital are attacked.
- **Injuries:** Over 300 students hospitalized.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 2 — The Day the Streets Changed (Student Confrontation)**.

### July 16, 2024 — Martyrdom of Abu Sayed
- **Event:** At Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur, English literature student **Abu Sayed** (25) stands with arms outstretched facing armed police lines. Police discharge multiple 12-gauge shotgun rounds at point-blank range, killing him.
- **Impact:** Video footage of Sayed's courage goes viral globally, sparking nationwide indignation. Six lives lost nationwide in a single day.

### July 17 – 18, 2024 — "Complete Shutdown" & Kinetic Force Escalation
- **Event:** Students declare a nationwide "Complete Shutdown". Private universities, colleges, and citizens flood arterial roads (Uttara, Rampura, Badda, Jatrabari).
- **State Action:** Police and paramilitary Border Guard Bangladesh (BGB) deploy tear gas, stun grenades, and live ammunition.
- **Martyrdom of Mugdho:** In Uttara, student **Mir Mahfuzur Rahman Mugdho** distributes water bottles to tear-gassed demonstrators (*"Pani lagbe, pani?"*) before being shot in the head.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 3 — A City on Edge (Police Escalation & Humanitarian Aid)**.

### July 18 (Night) – July 23, 2024 — Total Digital Blackout & Curfew
- **Event:** The government enforces a total shutdown of mobile data, broadband internet, and social media.
- **July 19 (Midnight):** Nationwide military curfew imposed with shoot-on-sight orders. Bangladesh Army armored personnel carriers (APCs) patrol streets.
- **Casualties:** Over 200 civilians killed during the internet blackout; thousands wounded. Over 10,000 citizens arrested in midnight sweeps.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 4 — Curfew & Covert Routes (Blackout & APC Patrols)**.

### July 24 – August 2, 2024 — The Red Revolution & Cultural Resistance
- **Event:** Citizens, artists, and overseas diaspora adopt red profile photos and create revolutionary wall graffiti (*"Swadhinotar Lal"*).
- **Movement Cohesion:** Despite detention and torture of student coordinators, decentralized community networks sustain food, medical supplies, and legal defense.

### August 3, 2024 — The Historic One-Point Demand
- **Event:** Hundreds of thousands assemble at Central Shaheed Minar in the largest peaceful rally in modern Bangladesh history.
- **Declaration:** The "One-Point Demand" is declared: unconditional resignation of the Prime Minister and systemic transition to democratic accountability.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 5 — Mass Mobilization & The Wall of People**.

### August 4, 2024 — Nationwide Non-Cooperation
- **Event:** The All-Out Non-Cooperation Movement begins. Citizens withhold taxes and utility payments. Clashes occur as ruling party loyalists attempt to suppress gatherings; over 90 killed nationwide.

### August 5, 2024 ("36 July") — The Fall & Rebirth of Bangladesh
- **Event:** The "March to Dhaka" sees hundreds of thousands march from all sides of the capital towards Shahbagh and Gono Bhaban.
- **Climax:** Armed forces announce they will not fire on the public. The Prime Minister resigns and departs by military aircraft.
- **Cultural Name:** Citizens designate August 5 as **36 July**, symbolizing that the spirit of July will not conclude until genuine democracy is realized.
- **Level Linkage:** **Level 6 — 36 July: Historical Turning Point & Epilogue**.
