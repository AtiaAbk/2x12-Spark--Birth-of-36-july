| Item | Where |

| Other mentions | `hud/WhereWindsMeetHUD.java` victory text mentions Shahbagh; `docs/location_reference_matrix.md` lists Shahbagh for Levels 3, 4 and 6, but Level 4 and Level 6 location names do not mention it ("Dhaka Curfew Zone — Blackout Corridor", "Dhaka University — Liberated Campus"). |

### Screenshot

`docs/screenshots/t3/l3_pond_ghat.png` (Level 3, baseline `pond_ghat`, looking south along the avenue to the campus gate in tear-gas fog). This is the closest the game gets to "Shahbagh". Barricades beyond the gate are hidden by fog at this distance. No baseline viewpoint exists outside the gate.

### Concrete differences

1. **The place does not exist in game.** The player never sees a road junction; "Shahbagh" is a red-brick campus gate with a wall on either side.
2. **Missing defining elements:** wide multi-lane junction, metro viaduct and station, footbridges, hospital blocks, traffic signals, rickshaws, CNGs and buses.
3. **Wrong relationship to campus.** In reality the junction is about 1.2 km north of Curzon Hall at the end of Kazi Nazrul Islam Avenue past TSC; the game puts "Shahbagh" at the south gate, 76 m south of the hall.

## 5. Uncertainty list

- Identity of each building around the junction in the photos.
- Where barricades and police lines stood on specific July 2024 days.
- Exact geometry of the metro viaduct and station relative to the junction.
- Whether the red footbridge from 2019 was still in place in July 2024.
