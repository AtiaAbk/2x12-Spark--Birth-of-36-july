## 1. CURZON HALL (কার্জন হল) — HERO LANDMARK

### Historic Overview & Architectural Style
- **Construction Period:** Foundation laid February 1904 by Lord Curzon; completed 1908. (Day UNCERTAIN: this file said 14 February; Wikipedia "Curzon Hall", citing Banglapedia, gives 19 February 1904. Completion 1908 per Wikipedia citing Prothom Alo, 28 Oct 2016.)
- **Architectural Style:** **Indo-Saracenic (Mughal-Gothic Revival)**. A sublime synthesis of traditional Mughal imperial architecture (inspired directly by Akbar’s Diwan-i-Khas at Fatehpur Sikri) and Victorian colonial civic engineering.
- **Source:** Banglapedia (National Encyclopedia of Bangladesh), National Heritage Archives, OpenStreetMap (`way 264272612`). (T3 note: Banglapedia and "National Heritage Archives" were not checked directly; the Banglapedia link is via Wikipedia. No specific National Heritage Archives document was identified.)
- **Orientation (T3):** the main entrance façade faces **north** (Commons photo titled "Curzon Hall - Northern Facade", Biswarup Ganguly 2015, CC BY 3.0; the OSM polygon's central projection is on its north side). The game's façade faces south. **No large central dome** exists on the real building; the centre is a square tower with small domed kiosks.

### Dimensions & Structural Data (RECONSTRUCTION / approximate)
- **Footprint (OpenStreetMap way 264272612):**
  - **Width (East-West):** **93.9 meters** (APPROXIMATE: bounding box of the community-traced OSM polygon way 264272612, longitudes 90.4014710°E to 90.4023927°E; re-computed from OSM API v16 on 2026-10-24. Traced from imagery, not surveyed; may not cover the whole complex.)
  - **Depth (North-South):** **47.6 meters** (APPROXIMATE: same OSM bounding box, latitudes 23.7271647°N to 23.7275945°N; includes projections, so it is not the block depth.)
  - **Storeys / Levels:** 2 primary storeys (Ground + 1st Floor) + raised rooftop terrace with decorative chhatri pavilions (`building:levels=3` on OSM). Total height ≈ **18.5 meters** (RECONSTRUCTION: no published height found; sum of assumed storey heights 1.2m plinth + 4.8m ground + 4.5m 1st floor + 2.5m parapet + 5.5m dome drum/cupola. The "dome" term is wrong for the real building: the top element is a tower with small kiosks.)
- **Façade Rhythm & Bays:**
  - **Central Hall / Portal (Iwan):** Deep projecting central portal arch flanked by octagonal turrets terminating in domed chhatris.
  - **Arcade Verandahs:** Continuous covered verandahs wrap around both ground and first floors.
  - **Arch Typology:** Alternating horseshoe arches and cusped multi-foil pointed Mughal arches supported on paired stone/brick columns.
  - **Brackets & Eaves:** Deep overhanging stone chhajjas (eaves) supported by decorative carved corbel brackets (*gokh* brackets).
  - **Rooftop Line:** Pierced jali parapet walls crowned by corner kiosks, domed chhatri pavilions with white plastered cupolas, and a stately central dome.
- **Color Palette & Materials:**
  - Deep terracotta red fired brickwork (Albedo: `#A23828` to `#8B2B1E`).
  - White lime plaster / cream-colored chunam trim and cornices (Albedo: `#E8E2D2`).
  - Glazed dark teak timber doors and iron-grille casement windows.

- **Type:** Historic rectangular masonry-curbed freshwater pond directly facing the southern façade of Curzon Hall.
- **Evidence status (T3):** **FICTIONAL / composite.** OpenStreetMap shows no pond in front of Curzon Hall; the nearest is Shahidullah Hall pond (way 67887007), about 180 m south-south-east and off-axis. Dimensions below are game RECONSTRUCTION only.

  - **Width (East-West):** **18.0 meters**.
  - **Length (North-South):** **24.0 meters**.

## 4. APARAJEYO BANGLA & ARTS PLAZA (অপরাজেয় বাংলা)

- **Location:** South-West of Curzon Pukur in front of the Faculty of Arts building (Kala Bhaban).
- **Sculptor:** **Syed Abdullah Khalid** (assisted by Hasina Ahmed, Syed Hamid Mocksood).
- **Inauguration:** December 16, 1979 (Victory Day).
- **Dimensions (T3 evidence status):**
  - **Total Height:** **5.5 meters (18 feet)** (CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE: Wikipedia "Aparajeyo Bangla" infobox gives 5.5 m / 18 ft with no citation; the Banglapedia and Liberation War Museum sources claimed here were not checked. Whether 18 ft includes the base is not stated.)
  - **Base Height:** 1.8 meters (6 feet) stepped concrete plinth (RECONSTRUCTION, no source).
  - **Sculpture Height:** 3.7 meters (12 feet) (RECONSTRUCTION, derived by subtraction).
- **Composition:** Reinforced cast concrete. Three figures embodying the 1971 Liberation War:
  1. A stalwart farmer-freedom fighter in the center with a rifle slung over his shoulder and a grenade in hand.
  2. A young student freedom fighter carrying a rifle.
  3. A resolute young woman carrying a first-aid medical satchel.

## 5. ANTI-TERRORISM RAJU MEMORIAL SCULPTURE (সন্ত্রাস বিরোধী রাজু ভাস্কর্য)

- **Location:** In front of TSC, about 66 m north of the TSC main block (OSM node 9150960977). In game it is south-west of TSC. See `LOCATION_RajuSculpture.md`.
- **Sculptor:** **Shyamol (Shaymol) Chowdhury** with Gopal Paul (Wikipedia "Anti Terrorism Raju Memorial Sculpture", citing Prothom Alo).
- **Inauguration:** September 17, 1997.
- **Subject:** Dedicated to student leader **Moin Hossain Raju**, martyred on March 13, 1992 while leading a peaceful anti-terrorism demonstration.
- **Composition & Scale:**
  - Composition of **8 figures** (count per Wikipedia citing Prothom Alo) standing shoulder-to-shoulder with arms linked. At least one is a woman in a sari (Commons photo, A.M.R. 2006, CC BY 2.5); the "7 men, 1 woman" split is UNVERIFIED. There is no central spire.
  - Material: White cement and reinforced cast aggregate over a circular tiered plinth (**height ≈ 4.8 meters** total: RECONSTRUCTION, no source; material also unverified. Real pedestal is a dark tiled tapering mass.)

## 6. TEACHER-STUDENT CENTRE — TSC (টিএসসি)

- **Architect:** **Constantinos Apostolos Doxiadis** (C.A. Doxiadis, Athens).
- **Construction Period:** 1961–1964 (UNCERTAIN: Wikipedia "Teacher-Student Centre, University of Dhaka" gives completion 1961; extensions in the 1980s).
- **Style:** **Tropical Modernism**. Inspired by traditional Bengali rural homestead layout centered around an open courtyard (*uthan*).
- **Defining Architectural Features:**
  - **Auditorium:** ~~Dramatic sloping red-tiled faceted pyramid roof.~~ **Contradicted by photos (T3):** the auditorium is a long segmental **barrel vault** in concrete with a red-grid end wall (Commons, Biswarup Ganguly 2015, CC BY 3.0). Small pyramidal-roofed pavilions exist on the covered walkways around the lawn.
  - **Double-Layer Butterfly / Umbrella Canopy:** Floating concrete canopy over the entrance and main student walkways supported by slender pilotis.
  - **Exposed Materials:** Red brick infill walls combined with board-formed exposed concrete beams and brise-soleil sun louvers.
  - **Greek Memorial:** Small square Greek memorial in the grounds. Date UNCERTAIN: this file said 18th century; Wikipedia says 1915. OSM way 932286147 tags it `memorial=stele`, `height=4` (unsourced).

## 7. CENTRAL LIBRARY (ঢাকা বিশ্ববিদ্যালয় কেন্দ্রীয় গ্রন্থাগার)

- **Location:** (T3) About 1 km north-west of Curzon Hall near Nilkhet Road (OSM way 295394032, "Central Library Building I", `building:levels=3`). The "west side of campus facing Arts Plaza" description is the game layout, not verified geography.
- **Architect (T3):** Muzharul Islam, c. 1955 (Wikipedia "Muzharul Islam", citing S. A. Gandhi, "Poetry in Architecture"). CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE. Real façade is a white concrete frame with glazing and perforated concrete screens, not red brick.
- **Style:** Multi-storey monumental civic building.
- **Defining Architectural Features:**
  - Enclosed **open-air internal quadrangle courtyard** providing natural light and ventilation.
  - Deep recessed vertical sunshades and large multi-storey reading hall windows.
  - Elevated entrance podium reached by broad ceremonial steps.

## 8. MADHUR CANTEEN (মধুর ক্যান্টিন) & HAKIM CHATTAR

- **Madhur Canteen:**
  - **Origin:** Originally built in 1873 as the **Durbar Hall / Skating Rink of the Dhaka Nawabs**; historical venue of the 1906 Muslim League foundation; later managed by martyr Madhusudan Dey ("Modhu-da").
  - **Architecture:** Distinctive hipped pavilion roof with broad overhanging verandahs, timber rafters, and decorative brick plinth. In front stands the memorial bust of Modhu-da.
- **Hakim Chattar:**
  - Traditional open-sided pavilion / gazebo with clay-tiled hipped roof, surrounded by stone seating ledges beneath historic banyan and rain trees.

## 9. SWADHINATA SANGRAM SCULPTURE GARDEN (স্বাধীনতা সংগ্রাম)

- **Sculptor:** **Shamim Sikder** (Ekushey Padak recipient).
- **Inauguration:** March 7, 1999.
- **Scale:**
  - **Central Tower Height:** **18.3–21.3 meters (60–70 feet)** (UNSOURCED: no citation was recorded; treat as RECONSTRUCTION).
  - **Base Width:** **26.2 meters (86 feet)** (UNSOURCED: treat as RECONSTRUCTION).
- **Description:** Bangladesh’s largest sculpture complex. Central rising column surmounted by the Bangladesh National Flag, depicting the 1952 Language Movement, 1966 Six-Point Movement, 1969 Mass Uprising, and 1971 Liberation War, flanked by over 100 subsidiary sculptures of historic figures.
