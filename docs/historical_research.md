## 1. HISTORICAL METHODOLOGY & CITATION STANDARDS

This game depicts the July–August 2024 student-led uprising in Bangladesh, culminating in the resignation and departure of the autocratic regime on August 5, 2024 (popularly termed "36 July").

To ensure absolute historical integrity and ethical game design:
1. **Primary Sources & Independent Human Rights Reports**:
   - United Nations Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR): *Preliminary Analysis of Recent Protests and Unrest in Bangladesh* (August 2024).
   - Amnesty International: *Bangladesh: Violent crackdown on student protests must end immediately* (July 2024); *Analysis of weapon and munition deployments* (July 2024).
   - Human Rights Watch: *Bangladesh: End Brutal Crackdown on Protests* (July 2024).
   - Local Investigative Journalism: *The Daily Star*, *Prothom Alo*, *Dhaka Tribune*, *New Age Bangladesh*.
   - Broadcast & Video Archives: Verified contemporaneous footage from Al Jazeera English, BBC News, AFP, Reuters, and citizen journalist repositories.
2. **Three-Tier Historical Classification**:
   - `[VERIFIED]`: Direct, corroborated historical facts, dates, official actions, casualty records, and public statements.
   - `[DRAMATIZED]`: Realistic reconstructions of verified environmental conditions (e.g. curfew checkpoints, hospital supply lines, tear gas dispersal) framed around gameplay objectives.
   - `[COMPOSITE]`: Fictionalized player characters and dialogue designed to convey authentic lived experiences without putting words in real people's mouths.

## 2. CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE & PRIMARY EVENT MAPPING

### Phase 1: Peaceful Inception & Quota Reform Movement (June 5 – July 14, 2024)
- **June 5, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** High Court Division reinstates the 30% quota for children and grandchildren of 1971 freedom fighters, sparking student discontent over systemic inequality in civil service recruitment (BCS).
- **July 1–7, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Students from Dhaka University, Jahangirnagar University, Jagannath University, and other public institutions initiate peaceful assemblies under the banner of "Students Against Discrimination" (*Boishammo Birodhi Chhatra Andolon*).
- **Locations [VERIFIED]:** Curzon Hall, Dhaka University Central Library, Nilkhet, Shahbagh, Raju Memorial Sculpture.
- **Atmosphere & Slogans [VERIFIED]:** Chants including *"Amar Shonar Bangla, Boishammo Mukto Bangla"* (My Golden Bengal, Free of Discrimination) and *"Medhar joy hok"* (Let merit triumph). Completely non-violent assembly.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 1 ("Where It All Began")**.

### Phase 2: Campus Confrontations & The Armed Assault (July 15 – 17, 2024)
- **July 14, 2024 (Late Night) [VERIFIED]:** Following derogatory rhetoric comparing student protesters to *Razakars* (1971 wartime collaborators), spontaneous outrage erupts across DU halls (Rokeya Hall, Shamsun Nahar Hall, Surja Sen, Zia Hall). Thousands march to Raju Sculpture chanting *"Tumi ke? Ami ke? Razakar, Razakar! Ke boleche? Ke boleche? Shwairachar, Shwairachar!"*
- **July 15, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Ruling party student wing (Bangladesh Chhatra League - BCL) and affiliate cadres, many donning motorcycle helmets and armed with sticks, iron rods, and machetes, launch coordinated assaults on unarmed student demonstrators at Dhaka University (VC Chottor, Raju Sculpture, Shahidullah Hall) and inside Dhaka Medical College Hospital emergency triage. Over 300 injured.
- **July 16, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** In Rangpur, Begum Rokeya University student **Abu Sayed** (25) stands alone in the middle of Park Mour road with arms outstretched in defiance; police shoot him at close range with 12-gauge shotgun pellets. His televised death becomes the galvanizing symbol of national resistance. Nationwide casualties exceed 6.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 2 ("The Day the Streets Changed")**.

### Phase 3: The Complete Shutdown, Police Clashes & Blackout (July 18 – 20, 2024)
- **July 18, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Students declare "Complete Shutdown" (*Sampurna Bangla Blockade*). Private university students (BRAC, North South, AIUB, UIU) and school pupils join en masse at Rampura, Badda, Uttara, Shanir Akhra. Police deploy excessive kinetic force, CS tear gas, and sound grenades.
- **July 18 (Night) – July 23, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Government enacts a total digital blackout, severing broadband, 4G mobile internet, and international phone circuits, cutting off 170 million people from communication and masking state violence.
- **July 18 (Uttara) [VERIFIED]:** Student **Mir Mahfuzur Rahman Mugdho** distributes water and biscuits to tear-gassed demonstrators chanting *"Pani lagbe, pani?"* (Need water, anyone?) before being fatally shot in the forehead.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 3 ("A City on Edge")**.

### Phase 4: Curfew, Military Deployment & Civil Disobedience (July 20 – August 2, 2024)
- **July 19 (Midnight) / July 20, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Nationwide curfew declared. Bangladesh Army troops deployed onto streets with armored personnel carriers (APCs). "Shoot-on-sight" curfew orders announced. Over 10,000 arrests in midnight mass sweeps.
- **July 21, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Supreme Court Appellate Division scales down quotas to 93% merit, but public outrage has shifted from quota reform to justice for over 200 murdered citizens and accountability for mass brutality.
- **Late July, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Citizens adopt red profile pictures, perform street art, write revolutionary graffiti, and hold silent vigils. Medical professionals and ordinary citizens provide clandestine relief and shelter.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 4 ("Curfew & Covert Routes")**.

### Phase 5: Mass Mobilization & The One-Point Demand (August 3 – 4, 2024)
- **August 3, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Central Shaheed Minar hosts one of the largest peaceful gatherings in Bangladeshi history. Protesters announce the singular **One-Point Demand**: unconditional resignation of the Prime Minister and her cabinet.
- **August 4, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** All-out Non-Cooperation Movement (*Osahojog Andolon*). Violent clashes reported nationwide as ruling party cadres attempt to break assemblies; widespread defiance with crowds occupying main thoroughfares.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 5 ("Mass Mobilization & The Wall of People")**.

### Phase 6: The Long March to Dhaka & "36 July" (August 5, 2024)
- **August 5, 2024 [VERIFIED]:** Coordinators advance the "March to Dhaka" to August 5. Hundreds of thousands of citizens from Gazipur, Narayanganj, Uttara, and Mirpur breach barricades and march towards Central Dhaka and Shahbagh.
- **Afternoon of August 5 [VERIFIED]:** Army leadership announces that forces will not fire upon the public. The Prime Minister resigns and flees the country by military helicopter.
- **"36 July" Cultural Meaning [VERIFIED]:** Citizens poetically designated August 1 as July 32, August 2 as July 33... and August 5 as **36 July**, affirming that the revolution born in July would not end until democratic freedom was reclaimed.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 6 ("36 July: Historical Turning Point & Epilogue")**.

## 4. ETHICAL SAFEGUARDS

1. **No Gratuitous Blood or Sensationalized Violence:**
   In adherence to game design standards and respect for victims' families, injuries are depicted through narrative context, posture, and medical triage aid rather than gore or dismemberment.
2. **Strict Peaceful Rules:**
   Attacking or harming unarmed civilians or innocent student peers results in immediate disqualification ("VIOLATION: Unprovoked violence against unarmed peers is strictly forbidden").
3. **No Slander or Fabricated Quotes:**
   Real individuals are quoted only from documented public records. Composite characters are clearly designated.
