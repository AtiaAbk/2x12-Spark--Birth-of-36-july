## 2. CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE & PRIMARY EVENT MAPPING

### Phase 1: Peaceful inception (5 June – 14 July 2024)
- **5 June 2024 [VERIFIED] (L1-01):** the High Court reinstated the 30% quota for descendants of independence fighters.
- **1–7 July 2024 [CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE] (L1-02, L1-03):**
  - Protests resumed on 1 July, with Shahbagh blockades on 2–3 July.
  - The "Bangla Blockade" began on 6 July, and Dhaka was largely at a standstill on 7 July.
  - The platform was Students Against Discrimination.
- **Locations [RECONSTRUCTION]:** Curzon Hall, Central Library and other campus settings in Level 1 are reconstructions. The sources place early-July protests on campus and at Shahbagh, but not at those specific buildings.
- **Slogans [DISPUTED / UNCONFIRMED] (L1-05):** "Amar Shonar Bangla, Boishammo Mukto Bangla" and "Medhar joy hok" are not found in any source read. They have been removed from game text.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 1 ("Where It All Began")**.

### Phase 2: The razakar remark and the campus attacks (14–17 July 2024)
- **14 July 2024 [VERIFIED] (L1-07):**
  - The PM's "grandchildren of razakars" remark was made at a press conference.
  - Students protested that evening, chanting "Who are you? Who am I? Razakar, razakar". The clause "Says who? … The dictator" was added later (OHCHR para. 34).
  - Hundreds of women at DU broke the night locks of their halls.
  - The romanised Bangla spelling is [CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE].
- **15 July 2024 [VERIFIED] (L1-08):**
  - Chhatra League supporters armed with machetes, axes, sticks, some pistols and wearing motorbike helmets attacked student protesters at DU.
  - That evening they stormed emergency units at Dhaka Medical College Hospital.
  - "More than 300 injured on both sides" (Daily Star).
- **16 July 2024 [VERIFIED] (L3-01, L3-03):**
  - In Rangpur, Begum Rokeya University student **Abu Sayed** raised his arms as police began shooting. He was shot at least twice with pellet-loaded shotguns from about 14–15 m. OHCHR calls this a deliberate extrajudicial killing.
  - At least six people were killed that day.
  - His age is [DISPUTED / UNCONFIRMED]: 23 per OHCHR, 25 per July Archive.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 2 ("The Day the Streets Changed")**, all [FICTIONAL GAMEPLAY] objectives.

### Phase 3: Complete shutdown, lethal force, blackout (17–20 July 2024)
- **17–18 July 2024 [VERIFIED]:**
  - A "complete shutdown" was called on 17 July.
  - From 18 July security forces switched to lethal force (OHCHR para. 43).
  - Tear gas was used against protesters across Dhaka. Exact Level 3 placement at the DU / Shahbagh approach is [CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE] (L3-06).
- **18 July (evening) – 23 July 2024 [VERIFIED] (L1-09):** a complete nationwide internet shutdown, following earlier mobile-data cuts around campuses.
- **18 July, Uttara [VERIFIED] (L3-04, L3-05):**
  - Student **Mir Mahfuzur Rahman Mugdho** (25) was handing out water to protesters when he was killed by gunfire from security forces, at around 17:50 on Rabindra Sarani (OHCHR Case 3).
  - His call was "পানি লাগবে কারো, পানি, পানি?" (Daily Star Bangla).
  - Wound detail is intentionally not reproduced. The game has no playable Mugdho scene. His name appears only at the Level 6 floral memorial.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 3 ("A City on Edge")**: water and first-aid objectives, all [FICTIONAL GAMEPLAY].

### Phase 4: Curfew, arrests, court ruling (19 July – 2 August 2024)
- **19 July (midnight) [VERIFIED] (L4-01, L4-02):**
  - A national curfew began and 27,000 soldiers were deployed.
  - Obaidul Quader said publicly that forces had "shoot on sight" orders (OHCHR paras. 44, 117).
  - The armoured vehicle model in Level 4 is a [RECONSTRUCTION] (L4-03).
- **Helicopters (L4-06):**
  - Tear gas from helicopters: [VERIFIED].
  - Gunfire from helicopters (19–21 July): [DISPUTED / UNCONFIRMED]. Not shown in game.
- **21 July 2024 [VERIFIED]:**
  - The Supreme Court capped the freedom-fighter quota at 5%, with quotas at 7% overall.
  - By then the movement was also demanding accountability for the killings (OHCHR para. 45).
- **Numbers [CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE] (L4-05):**
  - Police reported arresting 10,525 men and 25 women from 1 July to 4 August, and RAB a further 1,152.
  - OHCHR estimates about 100 deaths on 18 July and close to 300 on 19 July.
  - The earlier "over 10,000 arrests in midnight sweeps" and "over 200 murdered citizens" have been replaced with these figures.
- **26–28 July [VERIFIED]:** coordinators were held by the Detective Branch, and a coerced video statement was released on 28 July.
- **Removed:** "red profile pictures", "silent vigils", "clandestine relief". These are plausible, but no source for them was read in this pass. The relief scenes in Level 4 are [FICTIONAL GAMEPLAY].
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 4 ("Curfew & Covert Routes")**.

### Phase 5: The One-Point Demand (3–4 August 2024)
- **3 August 2024 [VERIFIED] (L5-01):**
  - At around 5:30 pm at the Central Shaheed Minar, coordinator Nahid Islam announced the one-point demand for the downfall of PM Sheikh Hasina and her government.
  - Crowd size is [CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE] (L5-02): Prothom Alo says "thousands", with the crowd spreading.
- **4 August 2024 [VERIFIED] (L5-03):** non-cooperation began, about 93–97 people were killed, and a "March to Dhaka" was called.
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 5 ("The Wall of People")**. The player stands with the assembly; the player does not announce the demand ([FICTIONAL GAMEPLAY], L5-04).

### Phase 6: March to Dhaka and "36 July" (5 August 2024)
- **5 August 2024 [VERIFIED] (L6-01):** hundreds of thousands marched towards central Dhaka. Police and armed Awami League supporters fired at protesters in many locations.
- **Army [CORROBORATED BUT INCOMPLETE] (L6-02):** the Army and BGB "largely stood by". The earlier "Army leadership announces that forces will not fire upon the public" is not a sourced quote.
- **Departure [VERIFIED] (L6-03):**
  - At around 2 pm she "was flown out of Dhaka by Armed Forces helicopter and then out of the country" (OHCHR para. 53). Reuters reports a landing at Hindon near Delhi.
  - Departure point and route are [DISPUTED / UNCONFIRMED] (L6-04).
  - This departure helicopter must never be conflated with the disputed helicopter gunfire of 19–21 July.
- **"36 July" [VERIFIED] (L6-05):** protesters kept counting July until their demand was met.
- **Death estimate [VERIFIED as estimate] (L6-06):** "as many as 1,400" deaths from 15 July to 5 August (OHCHR para. 58).
- **Campaign Role:** **Level 6 ("36 July")**:
  - The march, the wreath and the Bell of Freedom are [FICTIONAL GAMEPLAY] (L6-07).
  - The DU campus setting is a [RECONSTRUCTION].
  - No game text places the departure at any in-game location.

## 4. ETHICAL SAFEGUARDS

1. **No gratuitous blood or sensationalised violence.** Injuries are conveyed through narrative context, posture and medical aid. Wound details of real victims are not reproduced in game text or summary docs.
2. **Strict peaceful rules.** Attacking unarmed civilians or fellow students causes immediate disqualification ("VIOLATION: Unprovoked violence against unarmed peers is strictly forbidden").
3. **No fabricated quotes.** Real people are quoted only with wording found in a source listed in `RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`. Fictional characters are labelled [FICTIONAL GAMEPLAY].
4. **Real victims are never mechanics.** Abu Sayed and Mugdho are named only in memorial and epilogue text. They are never interactive, never a prompt and never a reward.
5. **Disputed claims stay out of the game.** This covers helicopter gunfire, the departure route and Abu Sayed's age.
