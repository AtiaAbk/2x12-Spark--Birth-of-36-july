## 1. NARRATIVE PILLARS & ETHICAL CODE

1. **Human Dignity Over Spectacle:**
   The July 2024 uprising was a momentous and painful chapter in modern Bangladeshi history. It must never be trivialized as a generic combat playground. Suffering, martyrdom, and injury are depicted with solemn respect and artistic restraint.
2. **Fictional Lens for Historical Truth:**
   The player experiences the world through a fictional protagonist—a quiet, principled student among hundreds of thousands of real peers. Real historic leaders, martyrs, and participants are never given invented dialogue. Any quote attributed to a real person must come verbatim from documented public recordings.
3. **Labels for Historical Transparency:**
   Every scene, journal entry, and memorial is explicitly classified:
   - **`[VERIFIED]`**: Documented fact corroborated by primary reports (UN OHCHR, human rights dossiers, public archives).
   - **`[DRAMATIZED]`**: Narrative scenes constructed to illustrate verified conditions and experiences.
   - **`[COMPOSITE]`**: Fictional character or dialogue representing the collective voice of thousands of students and citizens.
4. **Strict Prohibition of Violence Against Civilians:**
   The movement was founded upon peaceful democratic reform. Hitting, threatening, or harming innocent bystanders, fellow students, or unarmed citizens results in immediate mission failure.

## 2. CHARACTER BIBLE

### Protagonist: Rafi (রাফি) — [COMPOSITE]
- **Role:** Second-year Physics student at Dhaka University (Curzon Hall).
- **Appearance:** Simple navy-blue panjabi or polo, rolled sleeves, canvas messenger bag with notebooks, dark denim trousers, sandals/sneakers.
- **Personality:** Observant, gentle, deliberate, unyielding when injustice threatens those he cares for. He does not seek conflict; he seeks truth and equality.
- **Evolution:** From a passive observer studying for exams in the quiet verandas of Curzon Hall to an active participant standing shoulder-to-shoulder with his generation.
- **Key Phrase:** *"আমিও থাকব।"* ("I will be there too.")

### Supporting Characters (Campus Community) — [COMPOSITE]
1. **Amina (আমিনা):** Third-year Biochemistry student. Prone to action, organizing first-aid supplies, water distribution, and bandage kits. Fiercely protective of her peers.
2. **Tanvir (তানভীর):** Arts student from Kala Bhaban. Cultural organizer, writing wall slogans and managing the distribution of printed leaflets and demand sheets.
3. **Mofiz Chacha (মফিজ চাচা):** Veteran tea-stall owner near Curzon Pukur. Has witnessed student movements across decades (1971, 1990, 2024). Offers quiet wisdom, warm tea, and shelter when storms gather.

### Historical Figures & Martyrs — [VERIFIED]
- **Treated with Utmost Reverence:** Abu Sayed, Mir Mugdho, and the hundreds of fallen are commemorated through quiet monuments, historical journal entries, and archival exhibits. They are never portrayed as interactive game pawns. Their documented last acts (distributing water bottles, standing unarmed) are honored through solemn thematic motifs.

### Level 1: "Where It All Began" (যেখান থেকে শুরু)
- **Period:** Mid-June 2024 → July 14, 2024.
- **Setting:** Dhaka University (Curzon Hall, Pukur, Arts Plaza, TSC).
- **Tone:** Sunlight filtering through rain trees, scholarly calm transitioning into quiet resolve.
- **Gameplay:** Pure peaceful exploration, dialogue with classmates, reading placards, distributing leaflets, listening to speeches, and choosing to stand with the gathering.
- **Climax:** July 14 midnight march following derogatory state remarks. Rafi steps out onto the Curzon steps into the rain and declares: *"আমিও থাকব।"*

### Level 2: "The Day the Streets Changed" (যেদিন পথ বদলে গেল)
- **Period:** July 15, 2024.
- **Setting:** Curzon Hall to Shahidullah Hall & Dhaka Medical corridor.
- **Tone:** Sudden betrayal, danger, sirens, overcast monsoon sky.
- **Gameplay:** Evasion, avoiding aggressive helmeted groups, guiding panic-stricken first-year students to the sanctuary of the chemistry library, escorting wounded students toward medical triage.

### Level 3: "A City on Edge" (অস্থির নগরী)
- **Period:** July 16–17, 2024.
- **Setting:** Expanding beyond campus to Nilkhet, Science Lab, and Shahbagh.
- **Tone:** Somber grief following the martyrdom of Abu Sayed in Rangpur, righteous sorrow.
- **Gameplay:** Navigation through closed streets, finding alternative paths around blockades, documenting testimonies, safe dormitory evacuations.

### Level 4: "Voices Across the City" (অন্ধকারে সংযোগ)
- **Period:** July 18–19, 2024.
- **Setting:** Residential neighborhoods (Badda, Rampura, Dhanmondi).
- **Tone:** Total silence of the internet blackout; eerie stillness punctuated by sudden sirens.
- **Gameplay:** Physical message relaying, rooftop signals, finding drinking water, community mutual aid networks operating without digital communication.

### Level 5: "The Cost of Courage" (সাহসের মূল্য)
- **Period:** July 20–25, 2024.
- **Setting:** Dense urban alleys under strict military curfew.
- **Tone:** Tense, solemn, deeply compassionate.
- **Gameplay:** Humanitarian aid delivery, sneaking supplies past searchlights, caring for injured civilians in makeshift domestic clinics, collecting and preserving evidence.

### Level 6: "A Turning Point" (এক মোহনায়)
- **Period:** August 3–4, 2024.
- **Setting:** Central Shaheed Minar and Fuller Road.
- **Tone:** Overwhelming solidarity, vibrant red banners, unified national voice.
- **Gameplay:** Organizing large crowds, painting memorial murals, orchestrating non-violent logistics, witnessing the announcement of the One-Point Demand.

### Level 7: "After the Storm" (ঝড় শেষের ভোর — প্রতীকী ৩৬ জুলাই)
- **Period:** August 5, 2024 and aftermath.
- **Setting:** Returning to Curzon Hall, Raju Memorial, and the campus paths walked in Level 1.
- **Tone:** Reverent, reflective, dawn of hope tinged with deep remembrance for the fallen.
- **Gameplay:** Walking the transformed campus, viewing murals and flower offerings, visiting the newly erected memorials, unlocking the complete historical archive, reflective closing credits with primary documentation and full sources.

## 4. BILINGUAL DIALOGUE & SUBTITLE PRINCIPLES

- All character voice or in-world speech is authentically Bengali (বাংলা), rendered in high-legibility Unicode via **Noto Sans Bengali**.
- English subtitles appear in an elegant, unobtrusive smoked-glass bar below, with complete grammatical nuance.
- Authentic colloquial student dialect of Dhaka University (*"দোস্ত, ল্যাব শেষ করছিস?", "সবাই রাজুর দিকে যাইতেছে..."*).
