# Spark: Birth of 36 July — Complete Controls & Gameplay Guide

This document details all keyboard keys, mouse buttons, shortcuts, athletic abilities, and martial arts combat mechanics in **Spark: Birth of 36 July**.

You can access the in-game **Controls & Historical Field Codex** at any time by pressing **`[H]`** or **`[F1]`**, or via the Pause Menu (`[ESC]`).

---

## 1. Locomotion & Athletics

| Action | Primary Input | Secondary / Alternative | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Move Forward** | `W` | `Up Arrow` (↑) | Move forward relative to camera view. |
| **Move Backward** | `S` | `Down Arrow` (↓) | Move backward relative to camera view. |
| **Strafe Left** | `A` | `Left Arrow` (←) | Strafe left relative to camera view. |
| **Strafe Right** | `D` | `Right Arrow` (→) | Strafe right relative to camera view. |
| **Sprint / Run** | `Left Shift` | `Right Shift` / `R` | Free running sprint with unlimited exploration stamina. |
| **Athletic Jump** | `Space` | `J` | Realistic gravity jump arc. Clears benches, obstacles, and curbs. |
| **Mid-Air Double Jump** | `Space` (Double-Tap) | `J` (Double-Tap) | Kinetic impulse jump in mid-air for acrobatic rooftop access. |
| **Vertical Wall Clamber** | `Space` (Hold at Wall) | `J` (Hold at Wall) | Scale vertical building facades and vault onto raised plinths. |
| **Crouch / Duck (Bosepora)** | `C` (Toggle) | `Left Control` (Hold) | Low crouch to sneak or duck beneath high swing attacks. |
| **Tactical Evasion / Dodge** | `Z` | `Left Alt` | Martial arts evasion slip with invulnerability frames (i-frames). |

## 2. Technical Martial Arts Combat & Defense

> **Historical Context Note**:  
> In **Chapter 1 ("Where It All Began")**, students maintain strict peaceful solidarity. Combat strikes are disabled.  
> Combat unlocks in **Chapter 2 ("The Day the Streets Changed")**, **Chapter 3**, **Chapter 4**, **Chapter 5**, and **Arena Mode**.

| Combat Technique | Primary Input | Secondary Input | Mechanics & Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bare-Handed Punch** | `Left-Click` | `F` | Fast kinetic strike. Chains into a fluid Jab -> Cross -> Heavy combo. |
| **High Martial Arts Kick** | `Q` | `K` | High-impact roundhouse kick that staggers armored opponents. |
| **Low Dragon Leg Sweep** | `C` + `Left-Click` / `F` | `C` + `Q` / `K` | Low tripping kick that disrupts opponent balance and sweeps legs. |
| **Defensive Guard** | `B` (Hold) | `Right-Click` (Hold) | Raises arms in defensive posture; absorbs and mitigates 80% damage. |
| **Flash Parry** | `B` (Timed Tap) | `Right-Click` (Timed Tap) | Timed block (0.25s window) before impact; stuns attacker and breaks posture. |
| **Counter-Strike** | Attack during wind-up | Attack during wind-up | Striking while the aggressor winds up deals **1.5× interrupt damage**. |

> **Campus Rule of July**:  
> Unprovoked attacks against unarmed, peaceful students result in immediate mission disqualification.

## 4. UI, Pause Menu & System Shortcuts

| Action | Key / Input | Context |
|---|---|---|
| **Pause Menu** | `Escape` | In gameplay (freezes simulation, shows options). |
| **Close Menu / Dismiss** | `Escape` | In any modal, map, dialog, or codex overlay. |
| **Controls & Codex** | `H` or `F1` | In gameplay or selected from Pause Menu. |
| **Cycle Graphics Tier** | `G` (in Pause Menu) | Cycles `LOW` -> `MEDIUM` -> `HIGH` rendering quality. |
| **Cycle Tone Mapping** | `T` (in Pause Menu) | Cycles `ACES Filmic (Cinematic)` -> `Extended Reinhard` -> `Linear`. |
| **Restart Current Level** | `R` (in Pause Menu) | Re-initializes and restarts the active chapter. |
| **Return to Main Menu** | `Q` (in Pause Menu) | Returns to the chapter selection screen. |
| **Quit Game** | `X` (in Pause Menu) | Opens the exit confirmation dialog (`[Enter]` confirms quit). |
| **Take Screenshot** | `F12` | Captures high-res uncompressed PNG to `assets/screenshots/`. |
